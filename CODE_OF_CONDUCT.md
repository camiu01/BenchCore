# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Discuss implementation choices, not a contributor's personal worth. Treat
security reports, private drafts, screenshots, logs, and credentials with care;
do not disclose another person's private data in project discussions.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards and take appropriate,
fair corrective action in response to inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful behavior.

Leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation,
issues, and other contributions that violate this policy, and will communicate
reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This policy applies within all project community spaces and when an individual
officially represents the community publicly. Examples include using an
official project account or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
the maintainer at **lorenzo.camuso@gmail.com**. Complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders must respect the privacy
and security of the reporter.

For software vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); conduct reports
and vulnerability reports are different processes.

## Enforcement guidelines

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other unprofessional or
unwelcome behavior.

**Consequence:** A private written warning describing the violation and why
the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with those involved, including unsolicited contact with people
enforcing the policy, for a specified period. This includes community spaces
and external channels. Violating the terms may lead to a temporary or
permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate
behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication
within the community for a specified period. No unsolicited public/private
contact with those involved or enforcing the policy. Violations may lead to
a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, sustained inappropriate
behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement
of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from public interaction within the community.
